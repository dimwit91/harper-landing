The lane

Email, calendar, and her number. That is the job.

Install Harper. Connect Gmail and Calendar. She drafts, you send. Scheduling can go out with real times. Add SMS and voice when you are ready.

  1. She watches on a humane cadence

    Inbox and labels during waking hours. Quiet overnight. Personal spam, if you connect a second mailbox, on a light sweep. No 3 a.m. recaps.

  2. She sorts like an EA, not a filter

    Known people (you have written them, they are internal, or you are in a real back-and-forth) surface. One-way pitches, generic shares, and review-your-account mail do not. When she takes a thread, it stays with her until you actually need to decide something.

  3. She drafts in your voice

    She reads recent sent mail in that thread or with that person, then writes the way you write. Short when you are short. Direct when you are direct. Always signed Harper.

  4. Send classes are explicit

    Scheduling and reschedules may go out so a “does Tuesday work?” does not bounce back to you. Everything else is a draft in chat until you approve that class. You can add classes later. You never wake up to a personality you did not hire.

  5. You get the headline, not the play-by-play

    Ping when something is sent, booked, or needs you. No screenshots of every text. No “I checked the inbox” when nothing happened. Idle means silent.

Yours in a sitting

Install Harper. Then tell her how you work.

She ships with strong defaults. After install, walk these once. She asks one playbook question at a time and saves the answer.

Name

Keep Harper, or rename. Title stays Assistant. The rule does not change: she is not you.

Mailbox

Connect the work inbox she should send from. Optionally connect a personal mailbox for spam sweep and booking receipts. Default to work unless a thread is clearly personal.

Calendar rules

Name the work calendar versus any shared calendar. Spell out protected blocks: morning routine, kids, travel, out of office. Shared calendar is for events a partner actually needs to be on. Everything else stays on your calendar. She will cluster people-meetings and leave holes so outsiders can still book.

Draft vs send

Out of the box: scheduling may send; everything else waits. Lock more classes when you trust them (a standing pre-call note, a specific internal forward). Until then, she drafts here.

SMS number

Give her one dedicated assistant number. Not your cell. Not another bot’s line. First text in a new thread identifies her. Later texts do not sign off. Occasional emoji. No email-in-a-text. No em dashes.

Voice

Turn inbound voice on that same number when you are ready. She answers as Harper. Outbound calling stays off until you say it is live.

Openers

Paste yours, or start with the samples above and tweak. Inbound first reply, new outbound she starts, forwards, and internal coworker tone. Still sign Harper.

Keep teaching her

Timezone labels. Who counts as internal. Which newsletters to keep. Who should get conference and event mail. Quiet hours. Whether the morning priorities ping is a text, a chat, or both.

Questions

Before you add her.

Does Harper pretend to be me?

No. She is Harper. External people hear that on the first message. Later replies still sign Harper. She will match your phrasing; she will not put your name on a message she wrote as if you typed it.

What can she send without asking me?

Scheduling and reschedules, so booking does not stall. You can lock more send-classes later. Everything else is a draft until you say yes.

Will she flood my chat?

No. She pings when something was sent, booked, forwarded, or needs a decision. If a scan finds nothing, she stays quiet. Afternoon priority checks stay quiet unless the day actually changed.

Can she use my personal cell?

Give her her own number. Mixing her line with your phone, or with another specialist bot, is how identity breaks. Inbound voice, when you turn it on, is on that same assistant number.

What about a family or shared calendar?

She uses it only when a partner needs to be involved. Your work and solo personal items stay on your calendar so the shared one does not become a junk drawer.

How does she know what is junk vs. a real person?

The mailbox, not a CRM. If you have written them, they are internal, or you are in a real back-and-forth, they are known. One-way pitches, discount codes, and stranger domains stay filed. When she is unsure on a cold pitch, she does not promote it to your inbox.

What happens to mail I actually have to do?

It does not sit in Inbox as a guilt pile. She puts it on that day’s single Email action block, a numbered list with links, then files the thread. You work the calendar, not the unread count.

Can I add my own jobs later?

Yes. Coaching pre-call templates, extra phone-system watches, and one-off booking or refund hunts are recipes you add after install. They are not in the public default, because they are yours, not everyone’s.

Does she place outbound calls?

Not until you turn that on. Inbound voice answers as Harper. Outbound calling is off by default.

What if I ask her to do something that is not email, calendar, or her number?

She hands it back to your lead agent. That is the lane. That is how she stays good.

Will she ever email “please unsubscribe me” from my address?

No. One-click or silent unsubscribe links only. If the only option is sending mail as you, she skips it and flags it.

What about verification codes?

She never copies, reports, or uses OTP, 2FA, or bank codes that land on email or SMS. Those are yours.
